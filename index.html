Project Executive Summary & Architecture

Project Name: Heritage Treasures - UNESCO Data Analytics

Team Members: Avanthi, Madhava Sai, Praveen, Revanth

Problem Statement: UNESCO World Heritage raw data is stored across complex datasets, making it challenging for researchers, decision-makers, and stakeholders to rapidly prioritize conservation efforts or analyze regional trends.

Proposed Solution: A centralized, responsive web analytics engine using dynamic data processing to visualize heritage sites by category, country, region, risk level, and historical timeline.

Preprocessing & Analytics Engine Steps

  • Data Cleaning: Handled missing values in risk status and standardized regional naming conventions.
  • Category Encoding: Segregated sites into Cultural (Architecture, Cities), Natural (National Parks, Coral Reefs), and Mixed sites.
  • GIS Coordinates Processing: Extracted latitude/longitude coordinates for precise spatial mapping.
  • Aggregation & KPI Calculations: Grouped site counts by country and year of inscription to measure global growth trends.
